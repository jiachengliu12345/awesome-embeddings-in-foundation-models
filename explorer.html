GB = 10⁹ Bytes. GiB = 2³⁰ Bytes. Displayed values are rounded. Sub-byte results describe ideal bit packing.

What this leaves out

Projection and gate weights, quantization scales, codebooks, caches, activations, allocation and alignment overhead, and training state. Real formats may pad the theoretical result.

What storage cannot predict

This is not a GPU memory, FLOPs, latency, or model-quality estimate. Offloading changes placement and transfer; compression can add decoding work.
